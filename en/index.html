What is Freemasonry

A school of character across the centuries

Heir to the ancient guilds of builders, Freemasonry uses the tools of the trade as symbols for inner construction: the square of rectitude, the level of equality, the plumb of integrity.

Gathered in Lodges, Masons study, debate ideas with tolerance and devote themselves to charity. The institution is neither a religion nor a party: it welcomes men of different faiths who acknowledge a Creative Principle and wish to become better people.

Our values

The pillars that guide us

Six values that translate the symbolic teachings of the Order into the life of every Brother.

Fraternity

Bonds of respect and friendship between people of different backgrounds, professions and beliefs.

Integrity

Upright conduct as the plumb line of public and private life.

Knowledge

The study of philosophy, history and symbols as a path to enlightenment.

Self-improvement

Constant work on oneself, symbolized by the rough stone that becomes polished.

Service

Discreet, organized charity for those who need it most.

Responsibility

Commitment to family, country and the community we live in.
