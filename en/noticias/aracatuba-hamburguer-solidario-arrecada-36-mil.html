Araçatuba Lodges raise R$ 36.6 thousand at a charity burger night

PhilanthropyNorthwestern São PauloSeptember 30, 20262 min read

On the evening of September 25, 2026, three Masonic Lodges in Araçatuba and an academic fraternity joined forces to hold a charity burger sale. The result was impressive: R$ 36.6 thousand raised, according to the figures released by the organizers.

The preparation involved Brothers, their wives and children, and volunteers, who took turns in the kitchen, serving and making deliveries. The event also had the support of local merchants who donated supplies.

The full amount will go to social projects supported by the participating Lodges. For the organizers, the success of the night shows the strength of joint work among the region's Lodges.

Initiatives like this one put into numbers an ancient principle of Freemasonry: charity practiced with discretion, organization and responsibility.
