Our institution

About us

A Masonic Grand body committed to tradition, transparent governance and an active presence in society.

Mission

Making good men better

To promote the moral, intellectual and social improvement of its members so that they act with integrity in their families, at work and in the community.

Vision

A reference for ethics and service

To be recognized by society as a serious, transparent institution present in the causes of education, citizenship and philanthropy.

Values

Liberty, Equality, Fraternity

Tolerance, respect for the law, freedom of conscience, charity and love of country.

Our history

A history built Lodge by Lodge

The official history of the institution, with founding dates, milestones and key figures, will be published from the documentary archive and validated by the Secretariat of Culture and Memory.

In the meantime, learn about the general history of Freemasonry in Brazil and follow the archive digitization project.

Governance

Organizational structure

The organization follows the model of three independent powers common to Brazilian Masonic Grand bodies.

Executive Power

Grand Mastership

Administers the Grand body, coordinates the Secretariats and represents the institution.

Legislative Power

Assembly

Made up of representatives of the Lodges, it approves laws, the budget and the accounts.

Judicial Power

Courts

Rules on disciplinary matters and upholds the Constitution and regulations.
