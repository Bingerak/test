Heritage and memory

Preserving history for future generations

Minutes, photographs, banners, jewels and testimonies from veteran Brothers make up the institution's historical archive. The digitization project will make public what is authorized and safely preserve what is restricted.

Lodges and families who keep old documents can contribute to the archive.
